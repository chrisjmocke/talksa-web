Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information the Let's Talk app and website collect, why, who it is shared with, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Who we are

Let's Talk is run by LETS TALK SA (Pty) Ltd, a South African company, the "responsible party" for your information under POPIA. Contact us, or our Information Officer, at letstalktranslate@gmail.com. Our full company details are in our PAIA Manual.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google. You can also use the app as a guest without giving these.
  • A device identifier. Your phone's Android ID, used only so that the free trial is given once per phone.
  • Credits and payments. Your credit balance, the packs or subscription you buy, and payment references. Card details are entered on Paystack's page and never reach us.
  • What you translate. The text you type, speak or scan, and voice notes you upload, are sent to our server to be translated and spoken.

What stays on your phone

  • Your History and Learn phrases are saved on your phone, not on our servers.
  • Live speech is turned into text by your phone's own speech service before anything is sent to us.
  • Camera and photo scans are read on your phone. Only the text found is sent to us, not the picture.

Why we use it

  • To translate, transcribe and speak what you ask for.
  • To run your account, keep your credit balance correct and process purchases and subscriptions.
  • To prevent abuse, such as the same free trial being claimed many times.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell your information, show advertising, or use tracking or analytics tools in the app.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers ("operators") to run the app. Each only gets what it needs for its job:

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): sign-in, our database and our server.
  • Google Gemini: translation, voice-note transcription and language detection. We use Google's paid service, under which Google does not use your content to improve its products.
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech and Narakeet: turning translated text into speech. They receive the text to be spoken.
  • Paystack: card payments and subscriptions. Paystack receives your email address and payment details.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Information sent outside South Africa

Our server runs in Google Cloud's Johannesburg region, but some of the services above, such as Gemini and Narakeet, process text in other countries. We only use providers that are bound by data protection terms that give your information protection similar to POPIA.

How long we keep it

  • Account and credit records: while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete these within 30 days.
  • Payment records: as long as tax and financial laws require, usually five years.
  • Voice notes are not stored. They are transcribed and then discarded.
  • Translations. To make the app faster, short phrases and their translations and audio may be kept in a shared cache. This cache is not linked to your name, email or account.
  • Server logs used to fix problems are deleted automatically after about 30 days.

How we protect it

Information is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and stored in Google Cloud. Your balance and payments can only be changed by our server, and each account can only read its own data. Our API keys are kept on the server, not in the app.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your account and personal information;
  • stop using your information, where the law allows.

Email letstalktranslate@gmail.com from the address on your account. We reply within 30 days. Deleting your account also removes any unused credits.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

Let's Talk is not meant for children under 18 without a parent or guardian's permission. Buying credits requires an adult.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if how we handle information changes, and change the date at the top.
