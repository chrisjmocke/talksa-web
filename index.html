Four ways in, one voice out

However the words reach you, Let's Talk translates them and reads them out loud.

Hold to talk

Keep your finger on the button and speak. Pause to think if you need to; it keeps listening until you let go.

Type it

Write or paste text and get the translation on screen, then hear it spoken.

Scan a sign or letter

Point the camera at printed text, or pick a photo. The app reads it, works out the language and translates it.

Translate a voice note

Got a WhatsApp, Telegram or Signal voice note you can't understand? Upload it (up to 2 minutes) and hear it in your language.

Learn as you go

Send any translation to the Learn page and practise it later. Slow it down to 0.75x or 0.5x to catch every sound. Phrases in Arabic, Hindi, Mandarin and the other foreign languages come with a pronunciation guide, so you can say them yourself.

Words that matter

At the clinic or with official paperwork, the right word counts. Health and legal terms are guided by official South African term lists, so they come out the way they are used in those settings.

History at hand

Every translation is kept in History. Play it again for free, share it, or send it to Learn.

How to install

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone using the button above.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or your Downloads folder.
  3. If your phone asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager ("Install unknown apps"). This is normal for apps not from the Play Store.
  4. Tap Install, then open Let's Talk and allow the microphone when asked.

To update, download the latest APK the same way and install it over the old one. Your credits and history stay.

Questions

Is it safe to install an APK?

Download Let's Talk only from this website or our GitHub releases page. Every release is signed by us, and your phone checks that signature when you update. Your phone may warn you because the app isn't from the Play Store; that is expected.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Let's Talk is available for Android phones only.

Does it work without internet?

No. Translation and the spoken voices need a data or Wi-Fi connection.

What if I choose the wrong language?

For scans and voice notes, the app works out which language the text is in and corrects the "From" language for you.

Can I scan text in Arabic, Hindi or Mandarin script?

The scanner reads text in Latin letters (A-Z) only. For other scripts, type the text, hold to talk or upload a voice note instead.
